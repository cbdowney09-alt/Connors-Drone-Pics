Built for the street, not the office

Big buttons, one hand, bright sun, bad signal. Canvassers spend their time at doors, not typing.

🏠

Automatic door hangers

While clocked in, the phone's GPS lights up every house the canvasser walks up to. One tap marks the whole street as door hangers.

🗺️

One shared map

Everyone in your company sees every pin, colored by status or by who placed it, so nobody knocks the same door twice.

⏱️

Shifts and team metrics

Clock in and out from the app. Owners see hours, door hangers, hangers per hour and the route walked for every shift.

📶

Works with no signal

Download house data for the states you work in, nearly all of the U.S. Pins save on the phone and sync when signal comes back.

🧭

Routes and zones

Draw a neighborhood and get a walking route through the houses you haven't visited yet. Tag zones with the year the houses were built.

📊

Your data, your way

Export pins and shifts to CSV any time, or send door hangers and shifts straight to a Google Sheet.

Up and running in a day

No hardware, no training binder. If your crew can use a phone map, they can use KnoxCanvas.
